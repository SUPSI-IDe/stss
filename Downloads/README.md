# Downloads

## Description

This folder contains the documentation, datasets, software, fabrication files, and technical resources produced within the **Small Data Practices for Urban Dialogue** research initiative.

The research explores how citizens can actively participate in the production, interpretation, and discussion of urban knowledge through situated, everyday, and small-scale data practices. Rather than focusing on large-scale datasets and automated forms of analysis, the initiative investigates how data generated through lived experiences, observations, bodily interactions, and local knowledge can support reflection, dialogue, and civic engagement around urban issues.

Through participatory workshops, DIY sensing tools, mapping activities, data visualizations, and public engagement events, the initiative examines how data can function not only as a source of information but also as a medium for collective sense-making, discussion, and the imagining of alternative urban futures.

The materials contained in this folder document the methods, tools, activities, and datasets developed through this research.

## Folder Contents

### `DIY Urban Scraping`

Documentation, datasets, and technical materials related to a series of DIY urban data-collection prototypes developed by students from the **MA Interaction Design SUPSI** and the **BA Visual Communication SUPSI** during the **DIY Urban Scraping Workshop** held at the **SUPSI DACD FabLab** from **7–11 July 2025**.

The folder contains:

- `Entro-py`
- `Thermal-access`
- `Urban-overflow`

### `Pixel Urbani`

Documentation, datasets, software, and activity materials produced during **Pixel Urbani**, a participatory workshop held at **Studio Foce** as part of the **LongLake Festival Lugano 2025**, promoted by the **City of Lugano**.

The folder contains materials related to three participatory activities:

- `Data Mapping`
- `Data Plotting`
- `Gesture Tracking`

including datasets, interfaces, fabrication files, visualization tools, and methodological documentation.
