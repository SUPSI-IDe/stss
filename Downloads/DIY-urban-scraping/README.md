# DIY-Urban-Scraping

## Description

This folder contains the **DIY prototype documentation** and datasets generated through **DIY Urban Scraping**, a workshop held at the **DACD Campus, SUPSI FabLab**, from **7–11 July 2025**.

The workshop brought together students from the **MA Interaction Design SUPSI** and the **BA Visual Communication SUPSI** to design and develop experimental DIY tools intended for citizen use. Through a combination of rapid prototyping, digital fabrication, electronics, and sensing technologies, participants created devices that support participatory urban data collection and encourage collective sense-making of urban environments. 

The resulting prototypes explore how citizens can actively contribute to observing, interpreting, and discussing everyday urban conditions through situated data practices.

## Folder Contents

### `Entro-py`

Documentation and datasets generated by **Entro-py**, a prototype focused on documenting cigarette butts in public space and exploring relationships between littering practices and urban waste infrastructure.

### `Thermal-access`

Documentation and datasets generated by **Thermal-access**, a prototype that uses thermal sensing and image processing to explore **thermal accessibility** in urban environments. By visualizing surface temperature variations, the project highlights how heat exposure can influence the comfort and accessibility of public spaces for different users.

### `Urban-overflow`

Documentation and datasets generated by **Urban-overflow**, a prototype that employs AI-based image recognition and algorithmic heatmap generation to reveal patterns of waste overflow around urban ecopoints, supporting reflection on waste management and collection infrastructures.
